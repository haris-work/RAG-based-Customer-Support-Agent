# Limited Warranty Policy

## Coverage overview

NovaTech Store's fictional catalog includes a limited warranty against defects in materials or workmanship under normal, intended use. Warranty duration begins on the verified delivery date and is SKU-specific. This policy is a training simulation and does not represent a real manufacturer's warranty or override rights that may apply under law.

## What is covered

Subject to verification, the limited warranty may cover a failure caused by a defect in materials or workmanship when the product is used according to its instructions and within its published operating limits. A covered remedy may be repair, replacement with the same or an equivalent product, or another remedy determined by the Warranty team under the applicable policy. The chatbot must not choose or guarantee the remedy.

## What is not covered

- Accidental drops, impact, crushing, liquid ingress or environmental damage beyond the product's stated rating.
- Misuse, abnormal electrical supply, overloading, improper installation or use outside the published specifications.
- Unauthorized opening, repair, modification, firmware alteration or tampering.
- Cosmetic wear that does not affect intended function, ordinary battery-capacity degradation, consumables, lost items or theft.
- Damage caused by incompatible accessories or third-party equipment, unless investigation identifies a covered product defect.
- Products without verifiable proof of purchase or products whose serial/SKU information has been altered, subject to applicable law.

## Proof of purchase and claim information

A customer should provide the order number or marketplace receipt, SKU/model, approximate delivery date, a description of the issue and troubleshooting already attempted. Serial number or photos may be requested through an approved secure support channel. Never ask for passwords, full payment-card details or authentication codes.

## Claim workflow

1. Verify purchase channel, order/receipt, delivery date and SKU.
2. Check warranty duration from the verified delivery date.
3. Determine whether the reported symptoms indicate a safety risk. If there is smoke, swelling, sparking, burning odor or abnormal heat, stop use and escalate immediately.
4. Provide only safe, documented troubleshooting steps when appropriate.
5. If the issue remains unresolved or appears to be a covered defect, open a Warranty case.
6. Warranty staff review eligibility and may request diagnostics, photographs or return of the unit.
7. The team communicates the approved remedy and any shipping instructions.

## Warranty after the 30-day return window

The end of the standard 30-calendar-day return window does not automatically end warranty coverage. A product may still be eligible for a warranty claim within its SKU-specific term. Conversely, being inside the warranty period does not mean every kind of damage is covered.

## Marketplace purchases

For marketplace purchases, the customer may need to use the marketplace receipt and its support workflow. NovaTech may review a warranty claim when the purchase can be verified, but cannot promise that a marketplace-specific policy is identical to the direct-store process.

## Escalation

All disputed denials, requests for exceptions, suspected manufacturing patterns, repeated failures, safety concerns and requests for a specific remedy must go to Warranty/Product Support. The AI can explain the written coverage but cannot adjudicate a disputed claim.
