# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
> Concise answers for retrieval. Account-specific and current transaction data must still be retrieved from authorized systems.

## FAQ-01: Where does NovaTech Store ship?

The fictional direct store ships to the 50 US states and Washington, DC. It does not currently ship internationally, to US territories, APO/FPO/DPO addresses or freight forwarders.

## FAQ-02: How long does order processing take?

In-stock orders are generally processed within 1–2 business days after payment authorization and fraud screening, excluding weekends and company-observed holidays.

## FAQ-03: How long is standard shipping?

For the contiguous 48 states, standard carrier transit is estimated at 5–7 business days after carrier acceptance. Processing time is additional.

## FAQ-04: How much does standard shipping cost?

Contiguous US standard shipping is $6.95. Alaska and Hawaii standard shipping is $14.95.

## FAQ-05: When is standard shipping free?

For the contiguous 48 states, free standard shipping applies to merchandise subtotals of at least $75 after discounts and before tax. Alaska and Hawaii are excluded.

## FAQ-06: Is expedited shipping available?

Contiguous US expedited shipping is $14.95 with an estimated 2–3 business days after carrier acceptance. It is not guaranteed, and it is not offered under this policy for Alaska or Hawaii.

## FAQ-07: Does the shipping estimate include processing?

No. Processing is generally 1–2 business days; the carrier estimate begins after the carrier accepts the parcel.

## FAQ-08: Can I change my shipping address?

It may be possible before fulfillment begins, but it is not guaranteed. Once a parcel is packed or accepted by the carrier, changes may no longer be possible. A human/order-system review is required.

## FAQ-09: Can I cancel an order?

A cancellation may be possible while the order remains in a cancellable state. The order system must confirm cancellation; a request alone does not mean the order is cancelled.

## FAQ-10: Where can I find my tracking number?

A tracking reference is normally sent in the shipment email after carrier handoff and may appear in the signed-in order history. The AI must retrieve an individual tracking number from an authorized order lookup.

## FAQ-11: My tracking has not updated. What should I do?

Tracking can take time to show a new scan after carrier acceptance. Check the carrier link; if the shipment is more than 2 business days beyond its estimated transit window without delivery, support should open a Logistics investigation.

## FAQ-12: Tracking says delivered, but I do not have the package. What now?

Check nearby delivery locations, household members, building staff and any carrier photo/note. If it remains missing, contact support for a Logistics investigation. The AI must not declare theft or promise a replacement.

## FAQ-13: Do you ship to PO boxes?

Some carrier services may not support PO boxes. The checkout system determines which service and address types are available; the AI should not promise acceptance.

## FAQ-14: Can I return a product?

Most unused products in original condition and packaging may be returned within 30 calendar days after delivery, subject to exclusions and proof of purchase.

## FAQ-15: Is the return window 30 business days?

No. The standard return window is 30 calendar days after delivery, including weekends and holidays.

## FAQ-16: Can I return an opened product?

Opened products may be accepted when complete and undamaged. Eligibility depends on product type, condition and exclusions; Returns makes the final decision.

## FAQ-17: Who pays return shipping?

For a confirmed defect, wrong item or company error reported within 10 calendar days after delivery, NovaTech provides a prepaid label after review. For buyer-remorse returns, the customer pays return shipping.

## FAQ-18: What is the return-label fee?

A $7.95 fee may be deducted when a customer chooses a NovaTech-provided label for a buyer-remorse return. It does not apply when NovaTech is responsible for the return.

## FAQ-19: How do I start a return?

Use the authenticated order page or contact support. The system/Returns team verifies the order and issues an authorization and instructions if eligible.

## FAQ-20: How long does a refund take?

After a return arrives, inspection normally takes 3–5 business days. Once approved, the refund is submitted to the original payment method within 5–10 business days; the issuer may take longer to post it.

## FAQ-21: Can you refund me to a different card?

No. Refunds are sent to the original payment method. If that method has changed or expired, Finance must review the payment-provider process.

## FAQ-22: Are original shipping charges refunded?

Original outbound shipping charges are not normally refunded for a change-of-mind return. A company error or other exception requires human review.

## FAQ-23: Can I get a partial refund?

A partial refund may be considered after inspection if accessories are missing or condition requirements are not met. Only the Returns team can decide the amount.

## FAQ-24: How long is the warranty on NovaRoute AX1800?

The NV-RTAX NovaRoute AX1800 Wi-Fi 6 Router has a 2-year limited warranty from delivery.

## FAQ-25: How long is the warranty on NovaVault SSD?

The NV-SSD1T NovaVault Portable SSD 1TB has a 3-year limited warranty from delivery.

## FAQ-26: Does warranty cover accidental damage?

No. Accidental damage, misuse, unauthorized repair/modification and use outside published limits are excluded from the limited warranty, subject to applicable law.

## FAQ-27: Can I make a warranty claim after 30 days?

Yes, the end of the 30-day return window does not automatically end the product-specific warranty. The SKU's warranty duration and coverage exclusions still apply.

## FAQ-28: What proof do I need for a warranty claim?

Provide the order number or marketplace receipt, SKU/model, approximate delivery date and symptom description. Additional evidence may be requested through an approved secure channel.

## FAQ-29: What payment methods do you accept?

The fictional direct store accepts major credit/debit cards, eligible digital wallets shown at checkout and NovaTech gift cards. Marketplace payment options are controlled by that marketplace.

## FAQ-30: Why is there a pending payment?

A pending entry may be an authorization rather than a completed charge. The actual transaction state must be verified through the payment system or your card issuer.

## FAQ-31: I was charged twice. Can you check?

Finance must compare the order and payment-provider records. Share the order reference and approximate transaction details through the secure support workflow; never send a full card number or CVV.

## FAQ-32: Can support ask for my credit card number?

No. Do not share full card numbers, CVV, PINs or bank credentials in support chat or email. Enter payment details only in the secure checkout/payment interface.

## FAQ-33: Do you offer student discounts?

No standing student-discount program is defined in this knowledge base. Current campaigns should be checked through the promotion system.

## FAQ-34: Can I combine two coupon codes?

Unless a campaign explicitly says otherwise, only one percentage/fixed-amount coupon may be used per order. Checkout displays the applicable discount.

## FAQ-35: Can you apply a coupon after I placed my order?

Retroactive coupon application is not guaranteed. A human may review the request, but the AI cannot promise an adjustment.

## FAQ-36: How do NovaPoints work?

Enrolled members earn 1 point per eligible whole USD spent on merchandise after discounts and before tax/shipping. Points are normally credited after delivery and review; the account ledger is authoritative.

## FAQ-37: What are 100 NovaPoints worth?

100 points may be redeemed for a $5 discount on an eligible direct-store order with at least a $25 merchandise subtotal, subject to program rules.

## FAQ-38: Do marketplace orders earn NovaPoints?

No. Marketplace orders are excluded from NovaPoints earning under this reference program.

## FAQ-39: How do I reset my password?

Use the storefront's Forgot password flow and follow the link sent to your registered email. Never share the reset link or verification code with support.

## FAQ-40: Can I check out as a guest?

Yes. Eligible direct-store purchases may be placed as guest checkout. Support can verify an order through the approved order lookup process.

## FAQ-41: How do I delete my account?

Submit an authenticated privacy request. The Privacy owner reviews it; some records may need to be retained. The AI cannot promise immediate or total deletion.

## FAQ-42: Does the USB-C hub work with every USB-C laptop?

No. USB data requires a compatible host, and HDMI output on NV-HUB8 specifically requires DisplayPort Alt Mode. USB-C connector shape alone does not guarantee video support.

## FAQ-43: Does the 100W cable support 4K monitors?

No. NV-CAB100 supports USB 2.0 data and charging but does not support DisplayPort video or Thunderbolt.

## FAQ-44: Will the 65W charger power my 100W laptop?

It may charge slowly or fail to maintain charge under heavy load. NV-CH65 is limited to 65 W and the laptop determines the negotiated power.

## FAQ-45: Does the Bluetooth mouse include a USB dongle?

No. NV-MSE2 connects through Bluetooth and does not include a USB receiver.

## FAQ-46: Can I use the keyboard wirelessly?

No. NV-KB87 is a wired USB-C keyboard and has no wireless radio or battery.

## FAQ-47: Can I swim with the Bluetooth speaker?

No. NV-SPK20 is IPX5 splash-resistant when its port cover is closed, but it is not designed for submersion. Do not immerse it or charge it while wet.

## FAQ-48: Does the 1080p webcam need a driver?

It is UVC-compliant and generally works with current supported operating systems without a proprietary driver. The video app still needs camera/microphone permissions.

## FAQ-49: Why does the 4K webcam only show 1080p?

The host may be using USB 2.0, or the video app may cap resolution. NV-CAM4K needs a USB 3.x path for 4K mode, and the app must support 4K.

## FAQ-50: Will the wireless charger charge my phone at 15W?

Only if the phone and charger negotiate a supported profile. NV-WC15's 15 W is a maximum; some phones charge at lower Qi rates.

## FAQ-51: Does the smart plug work on 5GHz Wi-Fi?

No. NV-SP2 requires a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. A dual-band router may work if it allows 2.4 GHz onboarding.

## FAQ-52: Can I use the smart plug with a space heater?

The plug is rated up to 15 A/1,800 W for resistive loads, but high-inrush or high-load appliances may be unsuitable. Do not use it if the appliance exceeds the rating; use the appliance manufacturer's guidance and escalate uncertainty.

## FAQ-53: Does the smart bulb work with a wall dimmer?

No. NV-BULB2 is not compatible with conventional wall dimmers. Keep the wall switch on for app control.

## FAQ-54: Does the indoor camera record without internet?

Local microSD recording may be available when a supported card is installed and recording is configured. Remote viewing requires internet connectivity.

## FAQ-55: Does the Wi-Fi 6 router include a modem?

No. NV-RTAX is a router and requires an existing modem/ONT or Ethernet handoff from the internet provider.

## FAQ-56: Can the SSD reach 1,050 MB/s on any computer?

No. That is an advertised maximum under ideal conditions. Peak performance requires a USB 3.2 Gen 2 host; slower ports reduce throughput.

## FAQ-57: Can the power bank charge my laptop?

It provides up to 20 W USB-C PD. It may charge some low-power devices, but laptops needing more than 20 W may not charge or may discharge while connected.

## FAQ-58: Can I use a product outside the United States?

The direct store currently ships only within the 50 states and Washington, DC. Product voltage, radio and regulatory compatibility outside the US is not established here.
